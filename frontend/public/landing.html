Core Capabilities

Built like a cockpit.

Every surface is engineered for focus — structural panels, monospaced telemetry, and a clear chain of command from task to resolution.

▦

Projects & Boards

Plan in hierarchies, execute on Kanban boards, and track work items with statuses, priorities, and assignments.

❖

Knowledge Vault

A markdown-powered wiki with folders, tags, backlinks, and a graph view — your team's institutional memory.

✦

AI Assistant

A local-first AI that searches your items and vault, creates work, and answers from your own data — your keys, your call.

⛨

Encrypted & Local

Data stays on disk. API keys are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM. Nothing leaves your network unless you choose.

⟳

Offline-Capable

A service worker keeps the console usable without a connection. Changes sync when you reconnect.

⚙

Self-Hosted

One binary or one Docker Compose file. Personal mode runs on SQLite with zero external services.

Deployment

One product, three scales.

Start alone, grow to a team, expand to the enterprise — same interface, same data model.

Personal

  • SQLite, single user
  • No Docker required
  • Runs on an old laptop
  • Auto-login option
Popular

Team

  • PostgreSQL + Docker
  • 2–20 users, roles
  • Real-time sync
  • Shared vault & boards

Enterprise

  • 20+ users, SSO-ready
  • nginx reverse proxy
  • Backups & audit
  • Priority support
