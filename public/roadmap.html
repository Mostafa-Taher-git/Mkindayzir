Evolution Blueprint · 2026 & Beyond

Next-Generation Operations.

Mkindayzir is constantly evolving. Below is the blueprint of upcoming capabilities — spanning customer-facing portals, knowledge automation, enterprise identity, and sovereign AI copilots.

Stage 1 · Support In Design

Dedicated Customer Portal SPA

A clean, isolated Single Page App (`portal.yourdomain.com` or `/portal`) allowing external customers to register, submit support requests, review tickets, and chat with your team in real time.

Customer model auth separate from internal users Shielded internal notes invisible to customers Direct ticket submission with drag-and-drop attachments
Stage 1 · Self-Service In Design

Public Knowledge Base

Publish selected runbooks, guides, and documentation notes directly from the Knowledge Vault to a fast, public-facing help center with instant full-text search and feedback voting.

Granular "Public Note" toggle in Vault Client-side fuzzy search across public articles Helpfulness rating & suggestion telemetry
Stage 1 · Channels Planned

Inbound Email Dispatcher

Turn incoming customer emails into support tickets automatically. Automatically reply with ticket confirmation numbers and route subsequent responses into the ticket thread.

IMAP / POP3 / SendGrid / Mailgun webhook ingestion Automatic header parsing & customer linking Rich HTML & Markdown body rendering
Stage 1 · Automation Planned

Automated SLA Enforcement

Define multi-tier SLA targets based on customer tier, priority, and ticket category. Real-time countdowns, warning notifications, and automated escalation to team leads.

Configurable business hours & holiday calendars Pre-breach warning notifications via WebSocket SLA compliance reporting & breach analytics
Stage 2 · Identity Planned

Enterprise SSO & Directory Sync

Connect your organizational identity provider — Google Workspace, Microsoft Entra ID / Azure AD, Okta, or Keycloak — with automated group-to-role provisioning.

SAML 2.0 & OpenID Connect (OIDC) protocols SCIM 2.0 user lifecycle synchronization Enforced 2FA & hardware security keys
Stage 2 · Governance Planned

Immutable Audit Vault

Append-only cryptographic audit logging tracking all administrative actions, data exports, permission elevations, and configuration changes for SOC 2 and ISO 27001 readiness.

Cryptographic hash chaining on audit logs One-click CSV/JSON compliance bundle export Log retention policies & remote syslog streaming
Stage 2 · Storage Planned

Multi-Destination Backups

Automate full and incremental backups of SQLite databases, PostgreSQL clusters, and media attachments to S3-compatible storage (MinIO, Wasabi, AWS, Cloudflare R2) and SFTP.

Client-side GPG encryption before upload Point-in-time recovery (PITR) support Health checks & automated backup verification
Stage 3 · AI Engine Research

AI Auto-Triage & Smart Copilot

On-premise or encrypted AI assistant that automatically categorizes incoming tickets, estimates priority, suggests relevant knowledge vault solutions, and drafts replies for agent review.

Local Ollama / vLLM or private OpenAI / Anthropic endpoints Semantic search across entire Knowledge Vault Strict human-in-the-loop approval mechanism
Stage 3 · Integration Research

Webhooks & ChatOps Automation

Bidirectional webhook engine connecting Mkindayzir with Slack, Discord, Microsoft Teams, GitHub, and custom webhooks for automated task creation and incident alert routing.

HMAC-SHA256 payload signature verification Configurable event filters & payload transformations Chat slash-commands for status queries and ticket updates
Stage 4 · Edge Research

Offline Mobile PWA

Installable Progressive Web App with background synchronization, IndexedDB caching for full offline work creation, and Web Push notifications for on-call personnel.

Service Worker background sync queue End-to-end encrypted local offline cache Push API notifications for assigned tasks and tickets
